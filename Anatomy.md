# Concept & Anatomy

**Higer-Regular** is a modern, high-contrast editorial serif typeface built for display headlines, luxury brand identities, and contemporary magazine layouts.

## Etymology & Conceptual Foundation

The name **Higer-Regular** brings together two complementary ideas:
* **Phonetic Elevation:** Echoing *"Higher,"* the name reflects vertical momentum, tall proportions, and editorial prestige.
* **Structural Permanence:** Rooted in the Old High German term *Häger* (meaning an enclosure, boundary, or guarded clearing), it signifies architectural discipline and deliberate form.

Visually, the typeface translates the stark geometry of high-altitude alpine terrain into letterforms: towering vertical stems, razor-sharp serifs, and dramatic light-to-shadow contrast reminiscent of sunlit snow ridges beside sheer glacial faces.

## Typographic Anatomy & Construction

Every glyph in Higer-Regular was drafted as vector paths in Adobe Illustrator and engineered in FontLab 8 with unified structural rules:

* **High Optical Contrast & Vertical Stress:** Thick primary stems transition sharply into delicate hairline crossbars and curves along a clean `0°` vertical stress axis, giving large display headlines crisp editorial authority.
* **Chiseled Serifs & Ink Traps:** Sharp, lightly bracketed serifs anchor each stem firmly to the baseline and cap line, supported by subtle `2.0` unit ink traps to preserve corner sharpness at high contrast.
* **Double-Storey Lowercase Architecture:** Classic double-storey `a` and `g` structures provide traditional editorial legibility and balanced counter spaces within tall, condensed-leaning proportions.
* **Sculpted Terminals:** Lowercase `f`, `j`, `r`, and `y` feature refined curved terminals that soften the angularity of the serifs without losing tension.
* **Integrated Ligature Joins:** Custom ligatures (`f_i`, `f_l`, `f_f`, `f_f_i`, `f_f_l`, `f_j`, `t_a`, `t_g`, `t_s`, `a_x`, `a_s`, `ij`) merge adjacent crossbars and terminals into single continuous strokes rather than letting glyphs collide.
* **Proportional Spacing System:** Sidebearings are governed by proportional formulas anchored to `H`, paired with class-based kerning pairs (`kern`) calibrated for tight editorial tracking.

## Font Master Dimensions & Vertical Metrics

Engineered on a standard **1000 Units Per Em (UPM)** grid:

| Metric | Value (Units) | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Units Per Em (UPM)** | `1000` | Standard PostScript/OpenType coordinate grid |
| **Ascender** | `980` | Tall vertical ascender clearance (`Safe top: 988`) |
| **Caps Height** | `872` | High uppercase proportion relative to UPM |
| **x-Height** | `610` | Generous lowercase torso height (~70% of Caps Height) for editorial readability |
| **Descender** | `-300` | Deep descender reach (`Safe bottom: -270`) |
| **Line Gap** | `0` | Tight default leading suited for multi-line display headlines |
| **Italic Angle** | `0°` | Upright editorial roman posture |
| **Curve / Corner Tension** | `60%` / `67%` | Smooth, controlled Bézier curvature across bowls and terminals |
| **Ink Trap Width** | `2.0` | Micro-notches at tight acute joins to keep sharp edges crisp |
